About the Forge

Inside this device, fragmentary stars wait on a slowly turning celestial sphere. None of them know their place yet. That is where you come in.

Astralith is a quiet tool for a quiet hour. Each level scatters five to twelve broken star points across a sphere of concrete and starlight. A ghost outline shows the pattern you must trace. Tap one star, tap another, and a right-angle tricarbon bond forges across the void. Match the target before your move budget runs out, and a constellation is born.

No account. No login. No analytics. No servers. Everything you forge lives on your device, and only your device.

The Three Pillars

Constellation Forge

Trace broken star points into patterns. Clean right angles, precise timing, and efficient moves earn bonus points and three-star ratings.

Photographic Lab

Turn any photo you capture or pick into a snapshot in your personal archive. Five palette tones re-tint your images from lunar silver to cobalt sapphire.

The Vault

Every forged constellation, every milestone glyph, and every daily record — kept locally on your iPhone. Yours to revisit, never uploaded.
