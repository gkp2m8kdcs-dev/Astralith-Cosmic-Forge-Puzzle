Astralith: Cosmic Forge Puzzle ("the App", "we", "our") is designed as a quiet, fully on-device experience. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App does and does not collect, store, or transmit.

Short version: The App does not collect personal data. The App does not transmit data to any server. Everything you forge, capture, or save stays on your iPhone.

1. Summary

Astralith: Cosmic Forge Puzzle is built around a simple principle: what you create in the App belongs to you, and only to you. There is no account system, no login, no analytics SDK, no advertising network, and no remote backend. The App does not require an internet connection to function.

2. Data We Collect

We collect no data. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, device IDs)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, or crash reports
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Contact lists, calendar entries, or health data
  • Any content you create inside the App (constellations, snapshots, milestones)

All gameplay progress, forged constellations, photographic snapshots, settings, and achievements are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage mechanisms. They are never transmitted off your device by the App.

3. System Permissions

The App requests only the minimum set of system permissions required for its creative features. Each permission is requested with the at the point of use and can be denied without affecting the rest of the App.

  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription) — used only when you choose to capture a photo inside the Photographic Lab. The App does not record video, does not upload images, and does not access the camera at any other moment.
  • Photo Library Read (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription) — used only when you choose to pick an existing photo for the Photographic Lab. Selected images remain your property and are not transmitted.
  • Photo Library Write (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription) — used only when you choose to save a forged constellation or transformed snapshot back to your photo library.
  • Microphone (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription) — the App does not record audio. This entry exists only to satisfy platform requirements; no audio data is captured, processed, or stored.

You may revoke any of these permissions at any time from your device's Settings app.

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not target children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from any user. Because the App collects no data at all, no special protections for minors are required.

5. Third Parties

The App contains no third-party analytics, no third-party tracking, no advertising SDKs, and no social network components. There are no third parties with whom we could share data, because we do not have any data to share.

6. Data Retention

All data created by the App is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App removes all of its locally stored data. We cannot recover your data for you, because we never had it.

7. Security

Because no data leaves your device, no data is exposed to network interception, third-party servers, or data breaches originating from our side. Locally stored data is protected by your device's standard security mechanisms.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we ever make a change to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, surface a notice inside a future version of the App. Because the App's design is to collect no data, any future changes will not retroactively affect information we never possessed.
